# 🍔 FOOD DELIVERY ANALYSIS DASHBOARD

### 📊 Business Performance & Sales Insights | Power BI

---

## 🎯 1. Project Overview

The *Food Delivery Analysis Dashboard* is an interactive Power BI business intelligence solution designed to provide management with a clear and consolidated view of food delivery sales performance.

The dashboard transforms raw food delivery data into meaningful business insights covering *sales, orders, customer ratings, quantity, city performance, delivery status, payment methods, delivery partners, product categories, monthly trends, and discount impact.*

### 💼 Business Objective

The primary objective of this dashboard is to help management:

* 💰 Monitor overall revenue performance
* 🛒 Track order volume and quantity
* ⭐ Understand customer satisfaction
* 🏙️ Compare sales performance across cities
* 🚚 Monitor delivery performance
* 💳 Analyze customer payment preferences
* 🤝 Compare delivery partner performance
* 🍔 Identify category-level sales opportunities
* 📈 Understand monthly sales trends
* 🎯 Evaluate the impact of discounts on sales

---

# 📌 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The top section of the dashboard provides four important KPIs for quick management-level decision-making.

### 💰 Total Sales — 791.40K

Total Sales represents the overall revenue generated from food delivery transactions.

*Business Insight:*
The Total Sales KPI gives management an immediate understanding of the overall revenue generated. It can be used as a primary metric for tracking business performance and comparing actual sales against future targets.

*Management Focus:*
Revenue growth, sales targets, city contribution, category contribution, and monthly sales movement should be monitored continuously.

---

### 🛒 Total Orders — 984

Total Orders represents the total number of food delivery orders recorded in the dataset.

*Business Insight:*
Order volume indicates customer purchasing activity. Management can compare order volume with sales revenue to understand whether revenue growth is being driven by more orders, higher-value orders, or both.

*Management Focus:*
Increasing repeat orders, improving customer retention, and optimizing promotional campaigns can help increase order volume.

---

### ⭐ Average Rating — 3.39

Average Rating represents the overall customer rating associated with the food delivery transactions.

*Business Insight:*
Customer ratings provide an indication of customer satisfaction and service experience.

An average rating of *3.39* suggests that there is an opportunity to improve the overall customer experience.

*Management Focus:*

* 🍱 Food quality
* 🚚 Delivery experience
* ⏱️ Delivery timeliness
* 📦 Order accuracy
* 🤝 Delivery partner service
* 📞 Customer support

Improving these areas can potentially increase customer satisfaction and repeat purchases.

---

### 📦 Total Quantity — 1.94K

Total Quantity represents the total number of food items ordered.

*Business Insight:*
This KPI helps management understand the overall volume of products being purchased.

Comparing *Total Quantity with Total Orders* can also provide an indication of the average number of items included in each order.

*Management Focus:*
Management can use this information to identify opportunities for *combo offers, cross-selling, upselling, and larger-order promotions.*

# 🚚 4. Delivery Status Analysis

### 📊 Visual: Count of Order ID by Delivery Status

The delivery-status chart divides orders into:

* 🟢 Delivered
* 🟠 Pending
* 🔴 Cancelled

The dashboard shows approximately:

*Delivered:* 345 orders — 35.06%
*Pending:* 326 orders — 33.13%
*Cancelled:* 313 orders — 31.81%

### ⚠️ Key Business Insight

The relatively high share of *Pending and Cancelled orders* is an important management attention area.

A large proportion of orders not reaching successful delivery can directly affect:

* 💰 Revenue
* 😊 Customer satisfaction
* ⭐ Ratings
* 🔁 Repeat orders
* 🚚 Operational efficiency

### 🎯 Management Recommendation

Management should investigate the reasons behind pending and cancelled orders, including:

* Delivery delays
* Restaurant preparation delays
* Order availability
* Customer cancellations
* Delivery partner availability
* Payment-related issues

Reducing cancellation and pending rates can create a direct opportunity to improve revenue and customer satisfaction.

# 📈 5. Monthly Sales Trend

The monthly trend provides visibility into changes in sales performance throughout the year.

| Month    | Sales |
| -------- | ----: |
| January  |  129K |
| February |  114K |
| March    |  106K |
| April    |   97K |
| May      |  143K |
| June     |  120K |

### 💡 Business Insight

*May* represents the strongest sales month in the displayed period, while *July* shows a significant decline.

The trend indicates that sales performance is not constant throughout the year.

### 🎯 Management Action

Management should investigate the factors behind the May sales increase, such as:

* 🎁 Promotional campaigns
* 💸 Discount offers
* 📢 Marketing activities
* 🍔 Category demand
* 🏙️ City-level performance
* 📅 Seasonal demand

The same successful strategies can potentially be replicated during lower-performing months.

* Order value
